# Topological Data Analysis and beyond

I am **Marco Delgado Garrido**, a mathematician working at the intersection of **Topological Data Analysis, Representation Theory and Computational Topology**.

I am affiliated with the **Instituto de Matemáticas de la Universidad de Sevilla (IMUS)** at the **Universidad de Sevilla** and the **Depto. Matemática Aplicada I**, Sevilla, Spain.

My research focuses particularly on the **algebraic aspects of persistence theory**, with an interest in multiparameter persistence and its connections with representation theory and sheaf theory.

My broader mathematical interests include:

- **Topological Data Analysis**
- **Algebraic aspects of persistence theory**
- **Representation theory**
- **Computational topology**
- **Computational algebra**
- **Sheaf Theory**

## Research

My current research interests lie in the development and study of mathematical structures arising in Topological Data Analysis, particularly those connected with multipersistence theory and its algebraic foundations. I am also interested in the connections of multipersistence with sheaf theory and representation theory. My recent work is also focused in the adjacent field of computational topology, particularly in image analysis and topological properties of nD-pictures.

This website will contain my publications, research projects, teaching activities, talks, and other academic material.
